What the score means

The score is a heuristic, not a guarantee. It is useful because the same few visible leaks repeatedly block otherwise decent pages from converting traffic into money.

85-100

Strong baseline. The paid report should focus on sharper copy, proof placement, and small layout improvements.

70-84

Usable but leaky. One or two unclear decisions may be wasting paid traffic or launch attention.

50-69

Likely leaking. Fix the first screen, proof, CTA, or form path before pushing more people to the page.

0-49

High leak risk. Visitors probably cannot quickly tell why to act, whether to trust the page, or what happens next.

Checklist FAQ

These are the common questions people search before they buy more traffic or ask for landing page feedback.

What should I fix first?

Fix the earliest decision point first: headline outcome, first CTA, proof near the CTA, and what happens after clicking. Later sections matter less if the first screen loses the visitor.

Is this only for SaaS?

No. The same checks apply to SaaS demo pages, ecommerce product pages, lead forms, course pages, paid newsletters, and service landing pages.

Can this guarantee revenue?

No. It identifies page-level leaks. Traffic quality, pricing, offer strength, market timing, and fulfillment still matter.
