Revenue Leak Report: Acme Demo Page

Goal: more qualified demo bookings from founder-led SaaS buyers. Page type: SaaS demo landing page. Main issue: the first screen asks for a call before proving what the buyer learns.

58 clarity score
Primary actionBook a demo
AudienceOps founders with manual workflows
Top priorityRewrite CTA and move proof higher
1

The first CTA asks for time before explaining the call outcome.

Visitors see "Book a demo" before they know whether the call will diagnose a problem, show pricing, confirm fit, or force a sales process. That makes the click feel expensive.

FixChange the CTA to "Book a 15-minute workflow fit check".
Why

The button now names the time cost and the buyer outcome.

Verify

At 390px width, headline, proof, and CTA are visible without scrolling.

2

Trust proof appears after the first decision point.

The page asks visitors to act before showing any customer, metric, use case, or credibility cue. This is likely suppressing demo intent from buyers who are interested but cautious.

FixMove one metric or customer quote directly under the first CTA.
Why

Proof belongs next to the point where the visitor weighs risk.

Verify

The first CTA section should contain outcome, risk reduction, and action.

3

The mobile first screen hides the buyer's next step.

On a phone-sized screen, secondary navigation and long explanation push the action below the first visible area. Mobile visitors may understand the offer but miss the action path.

FixShorten the hero copy and use one primary CTA above secondary links.
Why

Mobile visitors need a faster decision path than desktop readers.

Verify

Use a 390px viewport and confirm no horizontal overflow or buried action.

Current hero copy Automate your operations with AI.

Generic category language. It says what the product uses, not what the buyer gets or why the demo is worth booking.

Suggested rewrite Find the manual workflow costing your team 5+ hours a week.

Specific buyer pain, measurable outcome, and a clearer reason to take the next step.
